Privacy Policy

Chilota Music Academy · Last updated October 12, 2026

Chilota Music Academy is a piano practice app for families. It is built to collect as little as possible. This page explains what the app handles and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Everything the app records is stored only on the device you use it on: the players you add (a first name or nickname and a color), each player's stars, scores and practice history, and settings such as piano size and speed. We do not have a server that receives this information.

Signing in

If you sign in with Google or Apple, the app receives your account ID, name and email address from them, and keeps them on your device to identify your family account. We do not send them anywhere. Guests can use the app without signing in; nothing is saved for guests.

Microphone

In Listen mode the app uses the microphone to hear which piano note is played. The sound is analyzed on your device as it happens. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off at any time in your device settings.

Children

The account holder should be a parent or guardian. Players are profiles under that account and only need a first name or nickname. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and no player information leaves the device.

Advertising

The free version shows one ad when a song starts. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because children use the app, every ad request is marked as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, is limited to ads rated for general audiences, and asks for non-personalized ads only. The app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising ID. Google may still receive limited technical information such as the device type, app version and approximate location (from the IP address) to show and count ads and prevent fraud; see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The ad-free plans are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether a plan is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID and the purchase receipt from the store, not your name, email or card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics.

Music

Songs are public-domain or openly licensed scores. When new songs are published, the app downloads them from our song library. These requests contain no personal information.

Deleting your data

Use Delete account on the player screen to remove your account, all players and all progress from the device. Deleting the app also removes everything.
